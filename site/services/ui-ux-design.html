How we work

From user research to design-system handoff — one continuous loop.

Five disciplines we run in lockstep so every screen is grounded in evidence and ships ready to build.

01

Discovery & user research

User interviews, contextual inquiry, stakeholder workshops and competitor teardowns. We focus on understanding the actual jobs users are trying to do and where current solutions fail them — before a single pixel is drawn.

02

Information architecture & user flows

Card sorting, sitemap definition and end-to-end user flow diagrams. Most usability problems are architecture problems in disguise — we fix them at the structural layer first.

03

Wireframing & low-fi prototyping

Greyscale wireframes test layout, content hierarchy and core flows quickly. We iterate fast at this stage when changes are cheap — never at high-fidelity when changes cost days of design time.

04

High-fidelity UI design

Pixel-perfect Figma designs aligned to your brand — balancing accessibility, clarity and delight. Every screen ships in light/dark mode, mobile/tablet/desktop and edge-state variants.

05

Design system & developer handoff

Component libraries, design tokens, spacing systems and Figma dev-mode handoff — built so your engineers ship faster and future design work compounds rather than restarts.

Measurement

Design is the input. User outcomes are the output.

Six KPIs we report on so design always ladders back to product and business outcomes — not subjective taste.

01

Task completion rate

% of users who finish a defined task without help — the single clearest signal of UX clarity.

02

Time on task

Median time to complete a flow — measured before and after redesign so improvement is provable.

03

Error rate

Wrong taps, abandoned forms and rage-clicks — surfaced via session replay and lab studies.

04

SUS score

System Usability Scale benchmarked pre- and post-launch — an industry-standard score, not vibe-based feedback.

05

Conversion lift

Sign-ups, activations and checkout completion — A/B tested where data volume allows, before/after where it doesn't.

06

Support ticket reduction

Volume of how-do-I tickets after launch — the cheapest, most underrated UX metric a product team can track.

What makes DigiVeritaz the best UI/UX design agency in Mumbai?
DigiVeritaz has shipped 100+ products and 50+ design systems across B2B SaaS, fintech, healthtech, e-commerce and edtech — making us the best UI/UX design agency in Mumbai for product teams that need research-grounded, Figma-native, WCAG AA compliant design. What UI UX design services does DigiVeritaz offer in India?
As a full-service UI UX design agency in India, DigiVeritaz offers user research (interviews, journey maps, competitor teardowns), information architecture, wireframing, hi-fi UI design, interactive prototyping, design systems (token-driven Figma libraries), accessibility audits (WCAG 2.2 AA) and developer handoff via Figma Dev Mode. Our UI/UX design services cover SaaS web apps, mobile (iOS & Android), e-commerce flows, marketing sites, internal tools and admin panels — from MVP to mature platform redesign.
How does a UI/UX design agency in Mumbai improve product conversion rates?
A UI/UX design agency in Mumbai improves conversion by identifying and removing the friction points that cause users to abandon — confused flows, unclear hierarchy, mismatched expectations between headline and landing experience. DigiVeritaz uses task completion rate, time-on-task, error rate and SUS score as measurable UX KPIs — then ties design changes to sign-up, activation and checkout conversion lift via A/B testing. Our UI services have delivered documented conversion improvements ranging from 18% to 40%+ across e-commerce, SaaS onboarding and lead-gen landing pages.
Do you only design or also handle development?
We are primarily a design agency, but we partner closely with development teams (in-house at clients, our engineering partners, or independent shops). For end-to-end product builds, we provide handoff-ready Figma files, design systems, and developer support throughout implementation.
How long does a typical UI UX project take?
MVP design typically takes 4–6 weeks; full product redesigns 8–12 weeks; complex multi-product platforms 12–20 weeks. Timelines depend on scope, research depth, number of platforms, and stakeholder review cycles.
Do you conduct actual user research or just design what we ask for?
Yes — research is foundational to everything we do. Without research, design becomes opinion-driven decoration. We conduct user interviews, usability testing, and behavioural analysis as part of every meaningful engagement.
How do you price UI UX design projects?
Most projects are priced based on scope and complexity rather than hourly rates. MVP design starts around ₹2.5 lakhs; full product designs range ₹6–15 lakhs; ongoing design retainers from ₹1.5 lakhs per month. We provide fixed-scope quotes after a discovery call.
Can you work with our existing design system or do we need a new one?
Both. We can extend and improve your existing design system, or build a new one from the ground up if your current system is fragmented or outdated. Building on existing systems is usually faster and preserves visual continuity.
