CMO Consultancy: When Your Business Needs a Fractional CMO

By DigiVeritaz June 20, 2026 8 min read

Most growing Indian businesses reach a stage where marketing becomes the bottleneck. The team is executing — ads are running, content is going out, SEO is being worked on — but there is no overarching strategy tying it together. Revenue growth has plateaued, customer acquisition costs are rising, and nobody in the room has the seniority to diagnose why or design a fix.

This is exactly the gap a fractional CMO fills. Not a full-time C-suite hire at INR 50–80 lakhs per year. Not a junior consultant who repackages generic advice. A fractional CMO brings the strategic depth of a seasoned Chief Marketing Officer at a fraction of the cost — working with your team part-time, on a retained basis, to build the marketing engine your business needs to scale.

What Is a Fractional CMO?

A fractional CMO is an experienced senior marketing leader who works with your business on a part-time or project basis rather than as a full-time employee. They own marketing strategy, lead cross-functional execution, set KPIs, and build internal capability — then step back once the systems are running independently.

The model became mainstream in the US and UK over the past decade and is now growing rapidly among Indian startups, D2C brands, and professional services firms that need strategic marketing leadership without the full-time cost.

5 Signs Your Business Needs a Fractional CMO

Not every business needs CMO-level input. But these five signals reliably indicate that strategic marketing leadership would unlock significant value:

  • Marketing spend is growing but revenue growth is not keeping pace.
  • You have a marketing team but no clear positioning, messaging, or campaign strategy.
  • Your customer acquisition cost has increased more than 30% year-on-year with no clear explanation.
  • Your brand looks and sounds different across different channels and materials.
  • You are preparing for a funding round and investors are asking about your marketing strategy.

Full-Time CMO vs Agency vs Fractional: Cost Comparison

A full-time CMO in India costs INR 40–80 lakhs per year in salary alone, plus benefits, equity, and the considerable time investment in hiring and onboarding. A traditional digital agency provides execution without strategy — they run campaigns but rarely challenge your positioning or build internal capability. A fractional CMO typically costs INR 1.5–5 lakhs per month depending on engagement scope, delivering senior strategic input at roughly one-quarter the cost of a full-time hire.

For businesses at INR 2–50 crore revenue, the fractional model almost always delivers a stronger return on investment than either alternative.

What a Fractional CMO Does in the First 90 Days

The first 90 days of a fractional CMO engagement are diagnostic and foundational. A well-structured onboarding covers:

  • Full marketing audit: channels, spend, attribution, and performance against benchmarks.
  • Customer and market research: ICP definition, competitive positioning, and messaging review.
  • Strategy development: go-to-market plan, channel prioritisation, and 12-month roadmap.
  • Team assessment: identifying skill gaps and recommending hires, agencies, or tools to fill them.
  • KPI framework: defining the metrics that will govern marketing decisions going forward.

How to Evaluate and Select the Right CMO Consultant

The right fractional CMO for a D2C brand is not necessarily the right one for a B2B SaaS company. Evaluate candidates on: category experience (have they built marketing for a similar business type?), channel depth (do they have hands-on expertise in your primary growth channels?), team leadership (can they coach and develop your existing team?), and commercial orientation (do they talk in revenue and pipeline, not just reach and impressions?).

Ask for case studies with specific revenue outcomes — not just campaign statistics. A great fractional CMO should be able to articulate the direct relationship between their work and business growth.

A fractional CMO delivers C-suite marketing strategy at approximately 20–25% of the cost of a full-time hire. For growing Indian businesses between INR 2 crore and INR 50 crore revenue, it is consistently the highest-ROI marketing investment available.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a fractional CMO?
A fractional CMO is a senior marketing executive who works with a business on a part-time or project basis, providing C-suite level strategy, leadership, and accountability without the cost of a full-time hire.

How much does a fractional CMO cost in India?
Fractional CMO retainers in India typically range from INR 1.5 lakhs to INR 5 lakhs per month depending on the scope of engagement, number of days per month, and the CMO's specific expertise and track record.

How is a fractional CMO different from a marketing consultant?
A marketing consultant typically delivers recommendations in a report. A fractional CMO takes ownership of execution — they lead the team, manage agency relationships, and are accountable for marketing outcomes, not just advice.

At what stage should a business hire a fractional CMO?
Most businesses benefit from fractional CMO input at the INR 2–50 crore revenue stage — large enough to have a marketing team and budget that needs strategic direction, but not yet at the scale where a full-time C-suite hire is justified.
