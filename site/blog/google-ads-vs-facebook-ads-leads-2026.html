Google Ads vs Facebook Ads: Which Gets You More Leads in 2026?

By DigiVeritaz June 11, 2026 8 min read

You have a monthly ads budget and two options — Google Ads or Facebook Ads. Both will grow your business when used correctly. Both will drain your budget when used wrong. So which one actually gets you more leads?

This guide gives you a definitive, data-backed comparison of Google Ads vs Facebook Ads in 2026 — covering cost, targeting, conversion rates, and a clear verdict by business type.

The Core Difference

Google Ads captures demand. Facebook Ads creates demand. This single distinction determines which platform is right for your business — and ignoring it is the most expensive mistake advertisers make.

Google Ads shows your message to people actively searching for what you offer right now. Facebook Ads interrupts people who match your ideal customer profile but may not be looking yet. Both approaches work — for very different business situations.

Google Ads in 2026

Google Ads reaches high-intent searchers at the exact moment they are looking for a solution. In 2026, the Google Ads ecosystem includes Search, Performance Max, Display, YouTube, Shopping, and Demand Gen campaigns.

  • Average CPC in India: ₹20 – ₹80 for service keywords
  • Average conversion rate: 3.75% across all industries
  • Best for: Services, B2B, local businesses, high-intent purchases
  • Biggest advantage: Demand capture — reach people ready to hire or buy now
  • Biggest challenge: High competition for commercial keywords drives up cost per click

Facebook Ads in 2026

Meta Ads reaches users across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and the Audience Network based on demographics, interests, and behaviour. In 2026, Meta's Advantage+ campaigns have significantly improved targeting precision and cost efficiency.

  • Average CPM in India: ₹150 – ₹400
  • Average conversion rate: 1.5 – 2.5% for lead generation campaigns
  • Best for: B2C brands, e-commerce, awareness campaigns, remarketing
  • Biggest advantage: Unmatched audience targeting depth and visual ad formats
  • Biggest challenge: Lower intent — users are browsing, not actively searching

Which Platform Should You Choose?

Choose Google Ads if:

  • You offer a service people actively search for — SEO, legal advice, accounting, digital marketing
  • You are a B2B business targeting decision-makers researching solutions
  • You need leads quickly — Google Ads delivers same-day results when optimised correctly
  • Your average deal size justifies a higher CPC

Choose Facebook Ads if:

  • You sell a visual product — fashion, food, lifestyle, e-commerce
  • You need to build brand awareness before capturing search demand
  • Your audience is B2C and skews younger — 18 to 35 demographic
  • You want to retarget website visitors and nurture them with sequential messaging

Use Both if:

  • You want a full-funnel strategy — Facebook builds awareness, Google captures the resulting search demand
  • Your budget is ₹50,000 per month or more — split testing across both platforms gives the most reliable cost-per-lead data
  • You run an agency or manage diverse client verticals
Google Ads achieves an average conversion rate of 3.75% for search campaigns versus 1.8% for Facebook Ads. However, Facebook CPM is often 3x lower — making it superior for top-of-funnel reach and brand awareness campaigns.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which has better ROI — Google Ads or Facebook Ads?
It depends on your business type. Google Ads delivers higher ROI for service businesses and B2B due to high search intent. Facebook Ads delivers better ROI for e-commerce and brand awareness due to lower CPM and superior visual targeting.

Which platform is cheaper in India?
Facebook Ads have a lower entry cost — campaigns start from ₹500 per day. Google Ads CPC for competitive keywords ranges from ₹20 to ₹200. Actual cost depends entirely on your industry and targeting settings.

Can I run both at the same time?
Yes, and for most growing businesses this is the recommended approach. Facebook builds awareness and handles retargeting while Google captures the demand it creates. Together they cover the full customer journey.

Which is better for B2B lead generation?
Google Ads is generally stronger for B2B because it captures active search intent. LinkedIn Ads is also worth considering for high-value B2B targets. Facebook works for B2B at the awareness stage.

How should I split my budget between the two?
Start with 60% on the platform that matches your primary objective. If lead generation is the goal, start heavier on Google Ads. Rebalance monthly based on CPA data from both platforms.

Conclusion

Google Ads wins on intent. Facebook Ads wins on reach and cost-per-impression. The most effective paid media strategies in 2026 use both — Google to capture buyers who are ready, Facebook to build awareness among those who are not yet searching. Start with the platform that matches your immediate objective, measure cost per lead rigorously, and expand from there.
