How DigiVeritaz Delivers 4x–10x ROAS for Clients

By DigiVeritaz June 29, 2026 6 min read

What 4x–10x ROAS Actually Means

For every ₹1 spent on advertising, DigiVeritaz clients generate between ₹4 and ₹10 in revenue. Across 60+ clients and ₹65 crore in tracked revenue, this is not a promise — it is a measured outcome. As a performance marketing agency in Mumbai, this is the standard we hold ourselves to on every account.

This guide walks through exactly how we achieve these results — the framework, the process, and the principles that separate high-performing campaigns from average ones.

Phase 1: Discovery and Diagnostic

Before we spend a single rupee of client budget, we audit everything. This includes:

  • Current ad account structure and historical performance data
  • Website conversion rate and user experience analysis
  • Customer journey mapping from first touch to final conversion
  • Competitor advertising analysis (what they spend, where, and on what)
  • Tracking integrity check — GA4, pixel health, event firing accuracy

This diagnostic phase typically reveals 3–5 high-impact optimisation opportunities before we even launch a single new campaign.

Phase 2: Strategy and Architecture

Every campaign we build has a clear KPI hierarchy: primary KPI (ROAS or CPA), secondary KPIs (CTR, Quality Score, CPL), and guardrails (maximum CPC, minimum impression share). This architecture ensures every decision at the campaign level connects to the business outcome.

Our data strategy team models CAC payback and LTV before setting target CPA — ensuring our performance targets are commercially viable, not just mathematically clean.

Phase 3: Precision Tracking Setup

One of the biggest reasons campaigns underperform is poor tracking. Most agencies launch campaigns and trust the platform's default attribution. We do not. Our analytics configuration process includes:

  • GA4 event taxonomy design and implementation
  • Google Tag Manager container build with server-side capabilities
  • Meta Conversions API for first-party signal accuracy
  • Custom Looker Studio dashboards with real-time data
  • Attribution modelling aligned to actual customer journeys

Clean tracking means clean data. Clean data means better decisions.

Phase 4: Creative That Converts

Ad creative is the single biggest variable in campaign performance. We run structured creative testing across multiple formats — static, carousel, video, Reels — with clear hypotheses for each test. Winners are identified through statistical significance, not gut feel.

Our creative briefs are built around the customer's core problem, not the brand's features. The best-performing ads across all our accounts speak directly to the pain point in the first three seconds.

Phase 5: Continuous Optimisation

ROAS does not plateau — it compounds with optimisation. Our weekly process includes:

  • Bid strategy review and adjustment based on conversion trends
  • Negative keyword pruning to eliminate wasted spend
  • Audience performance analysis and bid modifier adjustments
  • Landing page conversion rate optimisation based on heatmaps and session recordings
  • Budget reallocation from underperforming ad sets to proven performers

Real Results: Case Examples

  • JK Shah Classes: High-volume lead generation for CA/CS coaching programs with tight CPL targets across Mumbai, Ahmedabad, and Delhi.
  • Hyundai Elite i20: Targeted digital campaign delivering quality automotive leads through precision demographic and intent-based targeting.
  • Khyber Cement: Regional brand awareness campaign across Jammu & Kashmir with high-impression TVC amplification on Meta.

Why Mumbai-Based DigiVeritaz Outperforms Larger Agencies

Larger agencies spread their best talent across hundreds of accounts. At DigiVeritaz, every client gets a dedicated performance marketing team with direct access to senior strategists. We do not offshore your account to juniors — we build it ourselves.

Conclusion

4x–10x ROAS is not magic — it is methodology. Discovery, strategy, tracking, creative, and continuous optimisation executed with discipline and data. If your current agency cannot explain exactly how they will achieve your ROAS target, it is time to talk to DigiVeritaz.
