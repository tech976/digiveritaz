Google Confirms It: There's No AI SEO Hack — Just Real Expertise, Fast Sites & Local Reputation

By DigiVeritaz July 27, 2026 7 min read

Google has released its first official guidance on how AI-powered search features actually evaluate websites, and the message is clear: there is no special ‘AI optimisation’ trick. Fast, expert, well-reputed sites still win. For brands hoping AI search meant a new set of shortcuts, this is a useful correction. Here is what a genuine SEO agency in India should take from it.

What Google's Guidance Actually Says

Google's clarification states plainly that AI-powered search features reward the same fundamentals that have driven traditional rankings for years — genuine expertise, fast and reliable websites, and strong local or category reputation. There is no separate technical trick or hidden signal that AI search evaluates differently from traditional ranking systems. This directly contradicts a wave of 2025 and early-2026 content claiming proprietary AEO hacks that supposedly bypass standard SEO fundamentals.

Why This Matters for Indian Brands Right Now

Many businesses have been sold ‘AI search optimisation’ packages promising rankings through undisclosed techniques, at premium prices, separate from standard SEO work. Google's statement effectively confirms that this framing is largely marketing rather than a genuinely distinct discipline. Brands already investing in a credible SEO agency in India focused on expertise, site performance and reputation do not need a separate AI-specific engagement — they need to do the fundamentals well and consistently.

What ‘Real Expertise’ Means in Practice

Google's definition of expertise for AI search purposes tracks closely with its long-standing E-E-A-T framework — experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trust. In practice this means content written by identifiable people with genuine credentials, case studies with real outcomes, transparent author bios, and consistent factual accuracy. Sites that treat content as a volume game rather than a credibility exercise will continue to underperform regardless of any AI-era formatting tricks applied on top.

Site Speed Is Still a Ranking Factor, Not a Footnote

The guidance's emphasis on fast websites reinforces that technical SEO remains foundational. Core Web Vitals, mobile responsiveness and clean site architecture directly affect both traditional rankings and AI search evaluation. Many Indian mid-market sites still carry unresolved technical debt — bloated scripts, unoptimised images, slow hosting — that quietly caps their ranking potential regardless of how strong the content strategy is on top of it. Fixing this is squarely website development work, not a content-side task.

Local Reputation as an AI Search Signal

Google explicitly named local reputation as a factor AI search weighs, which elevates the importance of Google Business Profile management, local citations and genuine customer reviews — the same signals a disciplined online reputation management programme is built to strengthen. For brands searching for digital marketing services in Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore or any other Indian metro, local reputation signals compound with content quality to determine visibility in both traditional and AI-powered local search results.

The Practical Takeaway for Your SEO Strategy

Stop looking for AI-specific shortcuts and invest in the fundamentals Google has now explicitly confirmed matter: genuine expertise, fast technical performance and strong reputation signals. A disciplined SEO programme covering all three consistently outperforms scattered tactical experiments. Indian brands working with a results-focused SEO agency in India should expect their partner to prioritise these fundamentals over any packaged ‘AI SEO’ add-on that promises a separate mechanism.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there really no way to optimise for AI search?

There is no separate hidden trick, but there is a genuine discipline in structuring content, schema and expertise signals so AI systems can parse and trust it — that work builds on SEO fundamentals rather than replacing them.

Should I stop paying for AI-specific SEO services?

Evaluate what you are actually paying for. If it is genuinely structured content and schema work grounded in fundamentals, it has value. If it is marketed as a separate hidden technique, be skeptical based on Google's own guidance.

How important is site speed really?

Very. Site speed is one of the fundamentals Google explicitly named as mattering for AI search evaluation, alongside expertise and reputation, and remains a foundational technical SEO factor.

What is the fastest way to improve local reputation?

Focus on complete, accurate Google Business Profile information and a steady, genuine flow of customer reviews, which Google explicitly named as a factor AI search weighs for local visibility.
