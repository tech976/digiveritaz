You should have good knowledge of accounting, payroll, employee coordination, documentation and office administration. Salary is as per experience and industry standards.

Accounts & finance

  • Manage day-to-day accounting entries, invoices, receipts, payments and expense records
  • Prepare and maintain sales and purchase invoices
  • Handle vendor payments, client collections and payment follow-ups
  • Maintain bank, cash and petty cash records

  • Ensure timely communication of company policies and HR updates

Admin responsibilities

  • Manage day-to-day office administration and facility requirements
  • Coordinate office supplies, equipment, maintenance and vendor management
  • Maintain records of company assets, laptops and other office equipment
  • Coordinate with vendors and service providers
  • Ensure smooth functioning of office operations
  • Maintain important company documents and agreements
  • Support management with administrative and operational tasks as required

Required skills
