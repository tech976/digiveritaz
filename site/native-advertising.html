How we work

Five disciplines that turn native spend into qualified visitors.

Each runs in lockstep so the campaign compounds — instead of just spending budget.

01

Audience & content alignment

We start by matching the campaign content to the readers it should attract. We use interest segments, contextual signals and publisher categories. Native fails when the landing content doesn't earn the click. We make sure the read is worth it.

02

Headline & creative testing

Editorial-quality headlines and native-looking imagery, tested in batches of 15-20 at a time. We optimise for honest CTR and post-click engagement. We never run clickbait that destroys brand trust on landing.

03

Publisher whitelisting & brand safety

Native networks include both premium and very low-quality inventory. We curate domain whitelists and exclude misinformation categories. We also audit placements weekly. So your brand never lands next to content it shouldn't.

04

Bid strategy & frequency capping

Smart bid management at placement level. We push budget toward inventory that converts. We also frequency-cap, so the same reader isn't shown the same recommendation seven times in a session.

05

Reporting & conversion attribution

Server-side conversion tracking, post-click session quality and attribution back to leads or revenue. Monthly reviews show what worked and what we are cutting. They also show how next month gets sharper.

FAQs

Questions teams ask before they sign.

What are native advertising services and how do they work?
Native advertising services are managed campaigns that place brand content inside editorial environments — recommendation widgets, in-article units, sponsored editorial and in-feed placements — so ads match the look and feel of the surrounding content rather than interrupting it. A specialist native advertising agency handles network selection (Outbrain, Taboola, Colombia, Yahoo Native), headline and creative testing, publisher whitelisting, brand-safety configuration and post-click conversion tracking — so every rupee of media spend delivers qualified, engaged visitors rather than raw clicks.
What makes DigiVeritaz the right native advertising agency in India?
DigiVeritaz has shipped 240+ native campaigns across 80+ brands with Outbrain & Taboola certified strategists on every account. Our native advertising services include editorial-quality creative production, curated domain whitelists audited weekly, server-side conversion tracking, post-click engagement reporting (scroll depth, time-on-site, lead rate) and ASCI-compliant disclosure on every sponsored unit. Flat-fee management with zero ad spend markup means our incentive is always campaign performance — not budget bloat.
How do native advertising services compare to display advertising?
Native advertising services consistently outperform display for engagement and consideration metrics — native ads generate 53% more views than display ads, and consumers look at native ads 2× more than editorial content. Display wins on raw reach and retargeting scale at lower CPCs. The strongest programmes use both: native advertising services for content engagement and consideration, display for broad retargeting and awareness — managed by one native advertising agency so budgets are allocated to the channel earning each result.
Is native advertising the same as sponsored content?
They are closely related. Native ads appear in feed or recommendation widgets and link to an article or landing page. Sponsored content is a full editorial piece hosted on a publisher's site. Both form part of a native advertising strategy.
What budgets work for native advertising?
Native campaigns typically need a minimum of ₹50,000 per month to generate sufficient data for meaningful optimisation and reach.
How does native advertising compare to Google Display Network?
Native ads typically deliver higher engagement rates and longer time-on-site than GDN — because users are reading rather than browsing when they see them. GDN typically delivers cheaper raw clicks. Content engagement and consideration favour native; broad retargeting and awareness favour GDN; most sophisticated programmes use both.
How do you keep our brand off low-quality publisher sites?
Every campaign launches with a curated domain whitelist and category-level exclusions for conspiracy, misinformation and clickbait inventory. We audit placements weekly and remove low-quality publishers from spend.
What does a good native landing page look like?
A content page — not a homepage. Editorial structure, substantive read, and a soft CTA that follows the value the reader expected from the headline. Pages that match expectation convert; pages that bait-and-switch destroy brand trust.
