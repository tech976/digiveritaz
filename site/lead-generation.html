How we work

From ICP definition to scored pipeline — every month.

Five disciplines we run in lockstep so every lead in your CRM is one your sales team actually wants to call.

01

ICP definition & lead qualification spec

We sit down with sales and leadership to define the ideal customer, the disqualifiers, the MQL and SQL criteria. Everything downstream — copy, targeting, scoring — is calibrated to this single agreement.

02

Channel selection & budget allocation

Google Search, Meta, LinkedIn, programmatic, native, SEO — we model each channel against your ICP intent profile and ticket size, then split budget across capture, demand-gen and nurture in honest ratios.

03

Landing page & offer engineering

Conversion-optimised landing pages, lead magnets, gated content, calculators or demo offers — built around the buyer's question, not the brand's pitch deck. Forms tuned for the right friction, not the lowest.

04

Campaign launch & CRM wiring

Campaigns go live with full UTM hygiene, server-side conversions, offline-conversion uploads and CRM sync. Every lead lands in your pipeline with source, campaign and creative attribution attached.

05

Lead scoring, nurture & sales feedback

Behavioural and demographic scoring routes hot leads to AEs in minutes, lukewarm into nurture, cold into long-cycle re-engagement. Sales rates every lead — and that feedback rewires next month's targeting.

Measurement

Form fills are noise. Closed revenue is the signal.

Six KPIs we report on every month so lead-gen activity always ladders back to pipeline and revenue — not to the size of the lead list.

01

Cost per lead (CPL)

CPL by source, campaign and creative — the budget-side metric that decides whether a channel scales or gets paused.

02

Lead-to-MQL %

What fraction of raw leads meet the MQL definition agreed with sales — the first real quality filter in the funnel.

03

MQL-to-SQL %

How many MQLs sales accepts as genuinely sales-qualified — the metric that exposes whether marketing and sales actually agree.

04

Close rate

SQL-to-won percentage by source — the only number that tells you which lead surfaces are producing actual customers.

05

Pipeline value

Total open-opportunity value attributable to marketing-sourced leads — the metric your CFO cares about most.

06

ROAS / payback

Revenue divided by ad spend, plus blended payback period — keeps lead generation honest against the cost of acquiring it.

What lead generation services does DigiVeritaz offer in Mumbai?
As a lead generation agency in Mumbai, DigiVeritaz delivers the full lead engine — Google Search and PMax, Meta lead ads, LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms, native and programmatic, conversion-optimised landing pages, lead magnets, gated content, demo booking funnels, retargeting sequences, lead scoring, CRM wiring and WhatsApp + email nurture automation. Our lead generation services run capture, demand-gen and nurture as one connected programme — not six disconnected campaigns with no attribution between them.
How does a lead generation agency in India reduce cost per lead?
A specialist lead generation agency in India reduces CPL through four levers: tighter ICP targeting (eliminating audiences that never convert), landing page CRO (lifting CVR without raising bids), negative keyword and audience exclusion hygiene (stopping budget waste on disqualified traffic), and nurture reactivation (turning cold leads into MQLs instead of abandoning them). DigiVeritaz's lead generation services apply all four simultaneously — which is why our clients see an average 38% CPL reduction within the first 90 days of engagement.
How do you ensure lead quality?
We use campaign qualification (targeting intent-heavy keywords and audiences), landing page friction (filtering out non-serious enquiries), and lead scoring to ensure the leads delivered to your sales team are genuinely in-market.
Do you work for both B2B and B2C lead generation?
Yes. The channel mix and strategy differ significantly between B2B and B2C, and we have active experience in both — including regulated sectors like finance and healthcare.
How do you ensure leads are qualified, not just form fills?
We co-design qualification criteria with your sales team upfront — what defines an MQL or SQL, what budget thresholds matter, what signals indicate readiness. Lead capture forms, follow-up sequences, and scoring models are then built around those criteria. Plus we maintain feedback loops where sales rates each lead, informing campaign refinement.
What's a typical cost per lead in our industry?
It varies enormously. B2B SaaS leads typically cost ₹1,500–₹8,000 per qualified lead depending on category and target persona. Real estate leads ₹500–₹3,000. High-ticket consumer ₹300–₹1,500. We benchmark realistic targets after auditing your category data and providing competitive benchmarks.
How long until lead generation programmes show results?
Paid lead generation typically delivers leads within the first 1–2 weeks of launch, with quality and economics calibrating over 4–8 weeks. Inbound content programmes take 3–6 months to drive meaningful lead volume. ABM programmes show pipeline within 60–90 days but revenue contribution takes longer due to sales cycle length.
