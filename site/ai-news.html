Questions before you subscribe

What is the DigiVeritaz AI News digest?
It is a free daily email covering the most important digital marketing and AI news from the previous 24 hours — platform updates, algorithm changes, ad product launches and AI developments that affect how Indian brands market online.
When will I receive it?
Every morning at 10am IST, starting the day after you subscribe. Each edition covers the last 24 hours, so you can read it in a few minutes and know what changed.
Is it free?
Yes, completely free. We ask only for your name and email so we can personalise the digest and verify the address belongs to you.
Why do I need to verify my email?
We send a 6-digit code to confirm the address is yours. This stops people subscribing others without consent and keeps our list clean, which means better deliverability for everyone who genuinely wants the digest.
Can I unsubscribe?
Yes, at any time and with no questions asked. Every edition carries a one-click unsubscribe link at the foot of the email — open it and you are removed immediately. Cannot find an edition? Email info@digiveritaz.com and we will take you off the list.
